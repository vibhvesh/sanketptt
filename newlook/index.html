Built for distributed teams

From the field to the control room.

Teams may work with rugged handhelds, speaker-mics, hands-free headsets, smartphones, tablets or satellite communication equipment. Sanket is the software layer that brings supported PTT users, channels, recordings and operator dashboards into one coordinated workspace.

Connectivity depends on the device and network available at each location. Satellite links may provide a site's backhaul where supported; Sanket itself is not a satellite-phone service.

Practical starting points

Six practical ways to use Sanket.

Use cases based on the current PTT, transcription, Edge and SDR capabilities — start small, then add control-room and audio-routing hardware as needed.

01 · TEAM RADIO

Small teams & field coordination

Give field staff, supervisors and dispatchers a shared push-to-talk talk group from phones, tablets or PCs. Useful for maintenance, service crews, facility teams and on-site coordination.

02 · EVENTS & VENUES

Event operations & venue teams

Coordinate gate staff, stage managers, parking, housekeeping and security operations with named channels, call signs, presence and session playback.

03 · WAREHOUSE & CAMPUS

Warehouse, campus & plant operations

Connect stores, loading bays, maintenance and reception. Add a Sanket Edge Raspberry Pi listener to keep a local audio and transcript display running in a control point.

04 · RADIO DISPATCH

Control-room listening & voice logs

Monitor PTT traffic, replay recorded transmissions and create timestamped speech-to-text clips with keyword watch alerts. Useful for review, shift handover and searchable communication records.

05 · AMATEUR RADIO

Ham radio clubs & SSTV demonstrations

Run internet-connected club talk groups, practice net procedures and exchange SSTV images over the Sanket voice channel. Sanket PTT is an internet PTT app, not a replacement for a licensed amateur RF station.

06 · RECEIVER & PA

Receive-only RF monitoring & public address

Use Sanket Edge with a supported RTL-SDR to monitor permitted public or licensed-to-receive bands, or use Sanket आकाशवाणी to distribute operator announcements to connected USB audio outputs, amplifiers and speakers.
