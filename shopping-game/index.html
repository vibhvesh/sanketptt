How to play

  1. Choose your budget.
  2. Pick a grocery bay or use PTT to speak your order.
  3. Say the quantity and pack size: “10 eggs”, “200 gm apple”, “1 coconut”.
  4. Check the cart and running bill.
  5. Checkout. If you exceed the budget, the rocket launches and you lose!

“One bread” and “1 bread” mean one loaf. Eggs cost ₹6 per egg in this game.
